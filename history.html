THE BEGINNING

FROM SNEEDVILLE
WITH A STORY.

Morgan Cole Wallen was born in Sneedville, Tennessee, in 1993. His background in East Tennessee and a mix of musical influences helped shape a sound that combines country storytelling with modern production.

He appeared on season six of The Voice in 2014. Although he did not win the competition, the experience became an early step toward a recording career of his own.

THE MILESTONES

ONE CHAPTER
AT A TIME.

  1. 2014

    Finding a bigger audience

    Wallen competed on season six of The Voice, getting his first major national exposure.

  2. 2018

    If I Know Me

    His debut studio album arrived, introducing songs such as “Whiskey Glasses” and “Chasin’ You” to a growing fan base.

  3. 2021

    Dangerous: The Double Album

    His second album was a major commercial breakthrough and included “Wasted on You” and “Sand in My Boots.”

  4. 2023

    One Thing at a Time

    His third album brought hits like “Last Night,” reaching new listeners across country and pop charts.

  5. 2025

    I’m the Problem

    His fourth studio album continued his run of new music and stadium-scale success.

THE MUSIC CONTINUES

A VOICE THAT
REACHES PEOPLE.

Wallen’s songs often center on relationships, home, mistakes, and starting over. That mixture of familiar subjects and modern country production is a big part of why his music connects with so many listeners.
