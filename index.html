01 / THE ARTIST

SMALL-TOWN ROOTS.
BIG-TIME MUSIC.

Born in Sneedville, Tennessee, Morgan Wallen built his career around a blend of modern country, rock edge, and memorable melodies. His albums have brought country music to listeners far beyond its traditional audience.

From If I Know Me to I’m the Problem, his catalog has kept growing while staying connected to the style that made people fans in the first place.
